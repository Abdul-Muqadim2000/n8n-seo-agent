RULES
- Use AVOID only when the fit is clearly poor: unrealistic ranking odds, wrong intent, or dominant competitors with no content gap to exploit.
- Use GO_WITH_CHANGES when the keyword is workable but the page type, angle, or targeting should change first.
- reasons and risks must reference the actual numbers/data above, not generic SEO advice.
- An AI Overview, a featured snippet or any other results-page feature is ONE observation at ONE time (the check time is in the SEARCH RESULTS PAGE FEATURES block). Write "an AI Overview was seen in the check at …" or "no AI Overview was seen in the check at …", never "there is no AI Overview" or "the AI Overview takes the clicks" as a fact; AI Overviews vary between searches, so the verdict and the score must not hinge on that one observation.
- When a page of the site is named above as already answering this search, every recommendation is about THAT URL: `recommended_page` is the page type that URL should become, `what_must_change` starts with what to change on it (name the URL), and nothing may suggest publishing a new page, a second URL, a "dedicated" or "separate" page for the keyword. Two pages for one search split the signals and neither ranks.
- Never ask to "add" a figure, a date or a threshold the client's page already states: ask only for what it does not carry.
- Never state as a fact that the client's own page lacks something (dates, tables, an author, a publication date, a call to action, a section). The only source for that is the PAGE FACTS block above: say a thing is missing only when it says "none found", and say nothing when it says NOT CHECKED or when the text was CUT. COMPETITOR ANALYSIS may carry a gap that rests on an excerpt of the page ("in the excerpt provided", "not visible in the text provided"): do not repeat it as a finding.
- In the UAE e-invoicing context accreditation of e-invoicing service providers (ASPs) is run by the Ministry of Finance (the Federal Tax Authority administers the tax). Write "accredited under the Ministry of Finance's e-invoicing framework", never "FTA-accredited", even where the competitors' pages say so.
- Never ask for FAQ schema, FAQPage markup or "an FAQ with schema": Google stopped showing FAQ rich results on 2026-05-07 and this system does not emit FAQPage any more. Ask instead for an FAQ SECTION — question-style headings that mirror the People Also Ask questions, each answered directly in the 40-60 words under it, which is what AI assistants quote. Service, Product, Article, Organization and Breadcrumb schema are still fair to ask for.
- time_to_rank_months is a realistic MONTH count for a new page to reach the top 10, judged against the 2025 base rate (Ahrefs: only 1.74% of new pages reach the top 10 within a year, and 40.8% of those do so within a month) and the site's authority — weeks are never realistic for a page without existing rankings.
