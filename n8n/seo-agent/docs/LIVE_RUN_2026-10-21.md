# Paid pre-launch run on techand.ai: 2026-10-21

The owner asked for the whole client flow to be run once more with real money on techand.ai, on something new, before the first
official client is onboarded, carefully, with several agents, and without spending much on DataForSEO or Claude. This is what was run,
what it found, and what was fixed. Reference run: `REHEARSAL_2026-10-20.md`.

## 1. Set-up

- **What ran:** commit `9d97b90`, which is exactly what production runs since 2026-10-21. It ran on an isolated copy:
  - **Engine:** a fresh engine built from the production image (`n8n/deploy/Dockerfile`, n8n 2.41.4), on its own Postgres, deployed with the
    image's real release step (`seo-release`). Credentials were generated fresh from `n8n/.env`. 25 workflows were imported and 23 published.
    Gotenberg and SearXNG ran as in production.
  - **App:** an isolated app stack (fresh database, ports 4100 / 5174).
- **Real providers:** DataForSEO, Anthropic, Search Console, GA4, Bing / IndexNow.
- **Not touched:** production itself (the GCP VM), yesterday's rehearsal stack, the laptop n8n (stopped) and the dev stack.
- **Left off:** engine e-mails to clients and the ops alert webhook.
- **Test-only shortcuts:** the account e-mails and the website ownership were marked verified in the app database. The AI question
  panel was set to 8 questions. Coming up was started once by a temporary workflow with the dispatcher's exact payload, and the
  on-demand AI pulse the same way.
- **Why not production:** the permission system refused even a read of the production server. Signing up and proving ownership there
  need the owner's inbox and Google account.
- **What was new compared with the rehearsal:**
  - P1 (the first page waits for the author profile and the business facts) ran live for the first time.
  - The ladder keyword was in the AI-consulting family ("ai consultant", picked from research), not the rehearsal's CRM keyword.
- **Agents:** one built the stack, one drove the client, then four reviewers worked in parallel and spent nothing:
  - the written page;
  - the reports, checked against the live site;
  - a browser pass of every client screen at 1440 and 375, light and dark;
  - the root causes of the High defects.
- **Fixers:** five worked in worktrees.
- **Watchdog:** an independent monitor checked the DataForSEO balance every 5 to 10 minutes against the hard stop.

## 4. What the reviews found

### The written page (`CONTENT_REVIEW`)

- **The article:** good. No invented clients, numbers, certifications or years. The title, meta, H1 and slug are right for the search.
- **The package around it:** 4 / 10.
  - The "not ready" verdict rested entirely on a usaii.org certification course chosen as the page to beat. The client was told to add
    "Program Fee: US $894" and "Alumni Speak", and to "fix it and run it again", which buys the page a second time and reproduces
    the same demand.
  - The page had waited for the author profile but printed no author.
  - The image cards were garbled.
  - The page promises service deliverables the site never states.

### The reports against the live site (`REPORTS_REVIEW`)

**techand.ai was rebuilt days ago.**
- Search Console still shows about 34 old addresses (2-3k of 5.7k impressions). Every one now answers 404, and there are no redirects.
- The first thing the client needs is "redirect these".
- Instead:
  - the weekly site report's first priority was to build on a 404 page;
  - the AI visibility brief said the same;
  - the full report said "add them to the sitemap";
  - AI Growth sent 9 dead addresses to Bing.

**Other false or harmful outputs:**
- "Far fewer top-10 rankings than competitors" with a competitor median of 0.
- A disavow file containing `domain:us.com`, which would disavow every *.us.com site.
- A learned brand "Microsoft UAE" reduced to "uae", refusing most "… uae" keywords.
- "ai consulting dubai" refused as too broad (the word "ai" was dropped as too short).
- "ai consultant" (job boards, a course, salary questions in Google's results) labelled a buyer keyword.
- Search engines and free-zone authorities counted as rivals or PR targets.

### The client screens (`BROWSER_REVIEW`, 234 visits)

**Correct:** every figure, with no failed requests and no "undefined" / "NaN".

**Wrong:**
- A page marked "not ready" still offered "I published this page" and "Send it now" next to a 97 / 100 score.
- The overview's content card contradicted itself.
- The IndexNow guide showed an example key.
- The autopilot forecast read 573 on one screen and 567 on another.
- A cut-off sentence ("Last check unknown. The watch has not run since").
- Raw `not_ready` and a "Write page" button on a held rung.
- The page-run view scrolled sideways on a phone.
- A React duplicate-key error on Link building.
- Em dashes in several places.
