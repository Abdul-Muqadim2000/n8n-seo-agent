## 0. The owner's claim, checked

"We aren't doing any competitor analysis or research of what people search for to get a service."

- **Competitor analysis: the claim is wrong as stated, right in spirit.** The engine does competitor work in at least eight places
  (section 2): one shared competitor classifier `CMP` (`_competitors.js:15-182`), competitors' ranked keywords as a discovery source
  (`Competitor_Keyword_Requests.js:37-39`), a full-report "Competitor Benchmark" with a keyword gap and a backlink gap
  (`code_v4/Analyze_Keywords.js:891`, `code_v4/Build_Full_Report.js:1147-1263`), the verdict's read of the top 6 ranking pages
  (`Clean_Competitor_Pages.js`, `prompts/competitor_analyzer.txt`), rank-tracker competitor positions + share of voice
  (`_rank_serp.js:106-125`), backlink-gap prospects (`Gap_Requests.js`), AI share of voice vs rivals (`_ai_entities.js`, `AI_Metrics.js`).
  What is missing is a **competitor capability**: nothing finds, scores, stores and lets the client curate a competitor set; there is no
  competitor profile, no top pages, no topic/content gap, no domain-intersection keyword gap, no competitor alerts. Each path picks its
  own 3-5 competitors on the fly from 1-24 results pages and forgets them after the run.
- **"What people search to get a service": mostly right.** Candidates come from a Claude guess of seed phrases (`prompts/keyword_seeds.txt`)
  expanded by DataForSEO Labs (ideas / suggestions / related) plus, when the Site Tracker already stored them, up to 10 Search Console
  queries. Google Autocomplete is never called (no `autocomplete` anywhere in `v5/`, `build_*.py`). People Also Ask, related searches and
  forum threads ARE bought on every results page and cached (`_serp_cache.js:45-47`), but never become keyword candidates. Keywords with
  provider volume under 10 are dropped (`Collect_Research.js:44`), which removes exactly the local "X company in city" long tail that small
  markets have no volume for (`Assess_Response.js` R26 note: "business central partner uae", "dynamics 365 partner uae" had null data).

## 4. Selection-quality weaknesses, ranked by impact on the client

**H1 - Candidates come from a model's guess, not from what buyers type.** Seeds are Claude's phrases (`prompts/keyword_seeds.txt:13-22`)
plus declared services (`Seed_List.js:22`); the site is not read when the app sends business text (`code_v4/Normalize_Input.js:420-424`);
GSC seeds need stored Site Tracker rows (`GSC_Seeds_Discovery.js:15-17`), absent at setup, the moment the client picks "Your first
keyword". Labs ideas/suggestions/related only expand those seeds. No Autocomplete; PAA, related searches and forum threads are paid for
on every SERP (`_serp_cache.js:45-47`, `Clean_Competitor_Pages.js:121-123`) and only shown (4 PAA as a note, `Build_Keyword_Strategy.js:71`).
Questions = pool keywords that start with a question word (`Rank_Keywords.js:237`).

**H2 - The local service long tail is deleted.** Volume < 10 is dropped in discovery (`Collect_Research.js:44`), the ladder
(`Ladder_Pool.js:46`) and the full report (`code_v4/Analyze_Keywords.js` ideas, `if (!vol) return`). Labs has no volume for many real
UAE service searches (R26 note in `Assess_Response.js`). Only GSC-measured queries survive with `volume: null` (`Collect_Research.js:76-83`).

**H3 - Volume decides before fit.** Only the top 240 by `pre_score` reach the relevance screen (`Collect_Research.js:103`); pre_score has
no fit term (lines 85-100), and competitors' pulls are ordered by volume desc (`Competitor_Keyword_Requests.js:38`). A low-volume exact
service phrase can be cut before Claude or `service_fit` ever see it (service_fit is applied after, `Rank_Keywords.js:158-170`).

**H4 - Discovery competitors rest on one results page.** One SERP for Claude's first "local" seed (`Research_Requests.js:25-27`), so
`minSerps = 1` (`_competitors.js:148`) and the top 3 businesses of that page become the competitor set; their top-300-by-volume keywords
(rank <= 20) feed the pool. A broad competitor brings its broad/brand keywords (the A-24 failure mode, `Competitor_Keyword_Requests.js:4-9`,
is reduced by CMP but not removed). Declared competitors are capped at 2 (line 30). Nothing is stored for the next run.

**H5 - Discovery's "one page per search" is mostly the AI label.** `Rank_Keywords.js:176-178` compares only competitor URLs; a keyword needs
3 known top-10 URLs (`_kw_inputs.js:31`) and each competitor gives at most one, so a keyword is comparable only when all 3 competitors rank
top 10 for it; everything else falls to `topic label` (`KWI.pageGroup`, `_kw_inputs.js:401-404`). `Build_Keyword_Strategy.js:80-90`
recomputes clusters with 16 live top-10s but only tags keywords; `ks.clusters` (the content plan and its Now tiers, `Rank_Keywords.js:188-225`)
is never rebuilt, so two "Now" rows can be one results page. B2-7 open: discovery 3 of top 10 (`_kw_inputs.js:30,41-42`) vs ladder 3 of 7
(`Ladder_Clusters.js:103`, FIX_PLAN.md:1180).

**H6 - The verdict score is the model's.** `score 0-100` typed by the Verdict Agent (`prompts/verdict.txt:39`) drives the hold
(`Parse_Verdict.js:203-214`); the code itself documents a spread of 10 points on one keyword (`Parse_Verdict.js:199`). The deterministic
pieces (KD, reach, calibrated TP, page types, SERP evidence) exist on the same item and are not combined into the score. The verdict
path also never calls `fitGate` (section 1.9).

**M7 - "Wrong word" gets no measured better word.** Keyword Check alternatives are written by Haiku (`prompts/keyword_fit.txt:14`), offered
only when not realistic or fit 0 (`Assess_Response.js:153`), never volume- or difficulty-checked (lines 157-164). A valid but Hard keyword,
or one with no search data, gets no easier sibling. Same for content-run `secondary_keywords` (`prompts/verdict.txt:47`, `_verdict_own.js`).

**M8 - Full-report keyword gap is a join of samples.** Client and competitors are each 300 keywords by volume, rank <= 30
(`Ranked Keywords` body); `your_position` is null for anything outside the client's sample (`code_v4/Analyze_Keywords.js:74-76,99`), so
false gaps appear and counts never match provider totals (owner acceptance A3). Seeds are 3 fallback queries (`code_v4/Fallback_Queries.js`).

**M9 - "Difficulty for your site" ignores the results page.** It is Labs KD against the p75 KD of the site's own top-10 keywords
(`_reach.js:49-60,87-95`); unknown KD = reach + 10. No weakness signals (forums/UGC, thin or stale pages, local pack dominance). Only the
career rule (`fitCareer`) and page-type mismatch (`KWI.serpPageTypes`, said in text, `Build_Keyword_Strategy.js:121`, tier unchanged) read
the SERP; and only for the ~16 priority keywords.

**M10 - Intent has no confidence and is overwritten.** Labs `main_intent` (`Collect_Research.js:49`) is replaced by the AI label
(`Rank_Keywords.js:142-143`); regex fallback (`Collect_Research.js:22-29`). The live page-type evidence does not change intent or page type.

**M11 - The rules are tuned to one market.** `FIT_GEO`, `FIT_GENERIC`, `FIT_GOV_NAMES` (`_fit_gate.js:34-46`), CMP's gov / media host lists
(`_competitors.js:41-54`), `COUNTRY_WORDS` (~19 countries, `Competitor_Keyword_Requests.js:24`), `REACH_TOPIC_GENERIC` (`_reach.js:118-120`)
are UAE/GCC + ERP/e-invoicing heavy. Refusals and the local boost are weaker for any other market or industry.

**M12 - TP is a model.** `volume x click loss x 0.3` (`_kw_inputs.js:224`) or GSC impressions; never the top page's real traffic. Labelled
correctly (`traffic_basis`), but B5/B6 need a better TP.

**L13 - Three striking-distance rules** (B8 row above). **L14 - Six competitor exclusion lists**: CMP, `Pick_Top_6` SKIP, `Pick_Competitors`
BIG, `Gap_Requests.js:7-11` BIG, LB BIG (`LB_Page_Requests.js:15`, `Link_Merge.js:25`), dead `KWI.competitorClass`. The backlink-gap
competitor can be a directory. **L15 - Brand filter on the raw domain label** (`Seed_List.js:12,18`, `Collect_Research.js:14,25,101`) drops
every seed/keyword holding the label on a partial-match domain; the Keyword Check already uses `fitOwnLabels` (`Assess_Response.js:34-35`).
**L16 - Stale cost label**: `Rank_Keywords.js:304` prices live checks at $0.002, the request is $0.006 (`_kw_inputs.js:48`).
**L17 - Nothing is kept**: the pool, competitor sets and gaps exist only in the callback; Labs pulls are re-bought every run (only SERPs per
UTC day and reach 30 days are cached, `_serp_cache.js:1-13`, `_reach.js:65-77`). **L18 - `site_model.services_json` is not a seed source**
(`Seed_List.js:22` reads only `declared_services`).

Anything that makes a recommended keyword wrong for the business, in order: H3 + H2 (the right phrase never reaches the screen), H4 (the
pool is dominated by one competitor's world), H5 (two pages for one search), H6 (a page held or written on a noisy score), M9 (Easy on a
SERP the site cannot enter), M11 (another market's government/portal searches pass).

## 6. Safety: what must not change for existing production behaviour

1. **Callback shapes the app ingests**: stage `keyword_strategy` (`Build_Ideas_Response.js`: start_with, priority, content_plan, reach,
   quick_wins, questions, competitor_gaps, refused, covered, ladder_covered, search_console, serp_clusters, serp_overlap, live_checks,
   follow_ups), `ladder_plan`, `content` (verdict + hold), full report, and the synchronous Keyword Check body (`Assess_Response.js`, incl.
   `label`, `search_data_known`, `plan_assumed`, `alternatives`, `alternatives_held`). Add fields; never rename or drop.
2. **Request contract**: `shared/src/schemas.ts` / `payload.ts` key order (Normalize Input matches fuzzily), harness S23; the mode mapping
   `code_v4/Normalize_Input.js:185-205`; Rate Limit 40/day per company.
3. **Money**: run ledger reserve on the main path + settle at every end (`ladder_common.ledger_mainline`, build section 55 order proof); per-mode
   estimates `_cost_cal.js:216-240` (new modes need their own entries above measured); cost counted once by task id (R28); SERP day cache hit
   = cost 0 with `cache:<id>`; `free_only` reserves nothing; daily `ai_budget_usd` / `dfs_budget_usd` $15 and `dfs_balance_floor_usd`; the
   app quotes credits before a run and never charges above the quote.
4. **retry_lint**: 3 tries / 5 s on external HTTP nodes, but **no node-level retry on paid multi-item DataForSEO nodes** (N3, re-bills),
   the in-200 gate `_dfs_retry.js` instead (`retry_lint.py:20-60,252-273`).
5. **gate_lint**: fit gate on write (`fitGate`) and read (`fitStored` / `FITR.rungs`) of `seo_ladders`; link-spam and competitor
   (`OK.sellsSame`) pairs (`gate_lint.py:43-55`). A new table of keywords or competitors must join these pairs.
6. **lineage_lint**: every reported number carries measured / modelled / not available (INVARIANTS rule 1); `traffic_basis`, `__dfs`
   `unavailable` refusals (`code_v4/Pick_Competitors.js` competitors_evidence, `Analyze_Keywords.js` keywords_evidence).
7. **state_lint**: ladder states only advance on evidence (rule 4).
8. Frozen behaviour the harness pins: S25 (v4.7 discovery order when no services are declared, `Rank_Keywords.js:160-161`), S52 (KWI
   discovery inputs), S34 fit gate, the 137-case keyword-fit accuracy set; Cadence_Plan.js and `app/server/src/services/cadence.ts` change
   together; the Keyword Check's synchronous time budget (KWL-LAD D5, `Assess_Requests.js:28-29`).
9. Build hygiene: generated JSON only via `build_v4.py` (exit code checked), uuid5 node ids, secret scanner, `url_lint`, no `$env`, no URL
   constructor in Code nodes, Data Table columns only through `sync_tables.py`, engine deploy only after merging origin/main and a node diff
   against production (memory rule).
10. Rollout: new behaviour behind a per-site flag (off / shadow / on, as `ai_rank_mode`), off by default, so `discover`, `verdict`, `ladder`
    and the Keyword Check answer exactly as today until a site is switched.
