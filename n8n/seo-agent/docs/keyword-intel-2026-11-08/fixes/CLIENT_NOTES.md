A correction about the rankings report you received on 5 November.

  Console showed your page at about #25 on average for that search in the week up to 2 November. Pages near the bottom of the top 50

The corrected rankings are in your account now (the Rankings page and a new rankings report dated 8 November). Nothing on your
website needs to change because of this error. We are sorry for the confusion.
