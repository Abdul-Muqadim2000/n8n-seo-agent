What are the penalties for not complying with UAE e-invoicing rules?

Summaries of Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025 list six violation types. How do e-invoicing fines differ from existing VAT penalties?

E-invoicing fines and VAT penalties are separate regimes. VAT penalties, including late payment, fall under Federal Decree-Law No. 28 of 2022 on Tax Procedures. One untransmitted invoice can also create a VAT return mismatch, so both regimes can apply.

Who is exempt from e-invoicing, and are voluntary users fined?

Ministerial Decision No. 243 of 2025 defines the scope and lists the excluded transactions and entities. Check the list on the Ministry of Finance site, or with your tax adviser, before you rely on an exemption.

Are voluntary e-invoice users fined?

No. Businesses that join voluntarily before their mandatory date must follow the system's rules. However, the penalties in Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025 do not apply to them (source). Penalties start only once a business's mandatory deadline passes.
