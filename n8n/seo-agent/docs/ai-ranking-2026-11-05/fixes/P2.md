## 2. Corrections to DESIGN (the reasons are also in the code comments)

1. **L03 / L04 `mult` `'*': 0`.** Without it an unlisted engine took multiplier 1, so "add to Bing" would act on Google cells. L17 gets the same rule.
2. **L09 effort is 60 minutes, not 90.** R1 rates the profile effort "low".
3. **Eligibility look-ahead.** U(S1) - U(S0) is only 0.03, but S0 is a gate. Gain for L01-L04 adds the best next step from S1 among the levers always open to a site: L05, L11 and L12. Levers that need a cited page are left out because that page may not exist.
4. **Eligibility steps are exempt from the 0.02 minimum gain.** They are a gate and cost little.
5. **Own-page ranking chance.** An own page helps only if it ranks (R1 lever 2, E24). Its p is multiplied by `reach_w / 0.5`, clamped to [0.2, 1.5]. Unknown reach gives 1. On list-dominated questions the own-page chance is also halved (R1 section 7: the answers pick from the lists).
6. **L16 protect.** Its value is the loss avoided, U(S5) - U(S2). Under the DESIGN formula its gain was always 0.
7. **Site posterior is leave-one-out.** DESIGN G4 shrinks a site toward a global pool that already holds its own data, which counts that data twice. ARL shrinks toward the pool without the site's capped share.
   - Site rows store their raw totals as `alpha - prior_alpha` and `beta - prior_beta`, so learning is exact whether it runs at once or one outcome at a time (AL9).
   - The per-site cap of 10 applies to k and to n - k, exactly as in the DESIGN formula. `n_outcomes` counts actions.
8. **Who learns.** Site-wide levers (L11-L14, L17) and outcomes with no control background never enter learning. Eligibility levers learn with a background of 0.
9. **DESIGN A7 arithmetic is wrong for 20 / 10.** C(20,6)/C(30,6) = 0.065 is above 0.05, so k = 7 (p 0.038), not 6 (p 0.046). 16 / 8 gives k = 7 (p 0.033), as DESIGN says. **P1's AD5 expects 6 for 20 / 10 and must be corrected to 7.**
10. **Default weights.** `chatgpt_api` has engine weight 0 (DESIGN lists none for it).
11. **Own-page slot in shadow.** Shadow plans with the `on` caps (1 own page a week), so its plan is the one `on` would run. DESIGN said 0 in shadow. P4 can pass `capacities.own_pages: 0` to follow DESIGN literally.
12. **AL7 FPR scope.** It covers the full verdict path (DiD with the placebo, and Fisher when every treated question started at zero), not the bare DiD.

## 6. Second round (2026-11-06, after P1 and P4): AL14 on the real ARD, the production replay

After merging air/p1 (dbca1ceb, then e6c121d5) and air/p4, two things failed:
- AL14 failed on both archetypes.
- P4's offline replay of the private production dump chose **0 actions**: every gain was 0.0001-0.011, below the 0.02 floor.

### Root causes and fixes (items 13-17 continue section 2's list)

13. **Off-site levers were barred from S0 cells (ARL defect).**
    - ARD rightly marks nearly every cold-start cell S0: js_only for the non-rendering fetchers, not_indexed for Google and Copilot.
    - DESIGN 2.E's "S0: eligibility only" exists because content cannot help a page the engine cannot read. But L08-L14 and L17 put the name on a third-party page the engine *can* read.
    - Fix: they now act on S0 cells, priced from S1 (`effFrom`). Gain is measured from S0. Learning goes to the S1 prior (`prior_stage`). The background still comes from controls with the same real starting stage.
    - Own-site levers on S0 are still refused (AL3).
14. **ARD's additive `mapped_page` was not read (ARL defect).** Protection (L16) therefore had no URL. It is now read.
15. **Two own-page rules (ARL defects).**
    - A page that wins AI answers for one question is never rewritten for another question (DESIGN 2.F). That question gets a new page (L05) instead.
    - The list-dominated halving now applies only to recommendation questions. Its evidence (E26, R1 section 7) is about answers that choose providers from lists.
16. **The minimum gain was on the wrong scale (ARL, the replay's root cause).**
    - One well-placed cell is worth about w 0.3 x V 1 x F 0.5 x p 0.2 x ΔU 0.12 = 0.0036, so DESIGN's absolute 0.02 is about six such cells.
    - Its absolute size moves with V (no demand data: V 0.6-1.0) and F (devaicon's questions are in another market, F ≈ 0.08). Neither is calibrated yet.
    - A share of the panel's total headroom failed as well (tried): it grows with the panel and favours site-wide steps that add up many tiny cells.
    - **Fix:** the floor is on efficiency. An action must win at least 0.5% of what its own cells still have to win, gain / sum over its cells of w V F (U(S5) - U(s)). That does not depend on V, F or panel size.
    - An absolute floor of 0.0001 stays, and so does `capacities.min_gain` (absolute when given). Eligibility steps are exempt.
17. **Provisional cells (P1's new field, read as optional).** A provisional S0 / S1 (0-3 answers, read from the index or a rank proxy) is acted on with p x 0.8. An unmeasured cell gets no action.
