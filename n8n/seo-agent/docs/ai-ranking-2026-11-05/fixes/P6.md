## 2. The F-37 choice: the cadence honours AIR targets

**Problem.** AIR keeps a question of the client's own tracked AI panel. The cadence's global fit gate can call its keyword "off topic" or "too broad". The
target row is then queued, AIR's action is pre-registered, and the cadence would refuse to write it.

**Choice.** In the reserved slot, the cadence honours an AIR target:
- The gate's topic verdict does not apply to it (`off topic`, `too broad`), and neither does the level-2 topic-evidence requirement.
- Every safety refusal still refuses it, exactly as for any candidate: own brand, another brand, government, portal, job search, navigational, other market, empty.
- A safety refusal is listed in `refused` with its `target_id`, so AIR's next run and the app see why. The slot then goes to the next AIR target.
- Honoured targets are listed in `air.honoured`, and the page's reason says it was kept because it is one of the AI questions the client tracks.
- The global gate (`_fit_gate.js` and its app mirror) is not changed, and nothing outside the reserved slot is honoured (owner rule D13).

**Why not check the gate at plan time:**
1. The check would live in package P4's `AI_Rank_Plan.js`, which P6 must not touch.
2. More importantly, the plan cannot see what the cadence sees at write time: the ladders and pages written since, skips, the pile-up guard and the author profile. A plan-time check would still leave promises the cadence breaks. The write is judged where it happens, by one rule on both sides.

**Remaining gap.** A safety refusal in the cadence still means AIR queued a target the cadence will not write. It is rare, because panel questions are already gated for brands, and the refusal is visible.

**Tests.** `air_on_reserve_honours_topic` (an off-topic AIR target is written and listed as honoured, not refused) and `air_on_reserve_safety_refused` (a competitor-brand AIR target is refused; the next one is written). Both run in the engine (AE1) and in the app mirror.

## 4. Deviations and contract changes

- **D24 (CONTRACTS.md).** The three consumers inline AIRF. Two assertions in P4's `scenarios_air_plumb.js` were updated, because wave 2 changes the on-mode report by design:
  - AP4 now allows the on website's report and callback to gain the section, its lineage line and the key; everything else stays byte-identical.
  - AP11 lists the three consumers.
- **Node name.** The cadence load is named `Load AIR Cache (Cadence)`, not DESIGN's `Load AI Rank Protect (Cadence)`. P4's AIR_NODE pattern (`^Load AI Rank `) counts any matching node as an AI Growth node. The load sits before `Load Cycle Jobs (Cadence)` because build section R90 asserts `Load AI Brands (Cadence) -> Cadence Plan`.
- **Shadow shows nothing to clients.** The task said the report section is present "when the site is on or in shadow". DESIGN 4.2 and P4's AP3 shadow golden require shadow to change nothing a client sees, and the owner rule says shadow behaves exactly as before. So the section and the key are on-only. Shadow is visible to the owner in the app's platform-admin trial view and in `ai_rank_shadow_report.py`.
- **Kill switch in the Link Builder.** The Link Builder has no `seo_cache` kind-`air` load: adding one is P4's `build_monitors.py` beyond the `wire()` call. Rolling a website back there means setting its mode to off or shadow. The cadence and the report do honour the kill switch.
- **New directory checks.** The new entries are not fetched for evidence: `LB_Page_Requests.js` / `LB_Source_Requests.js` belong to no AIR package and call `listingsFor` without the option. They show the ledger and own-site evidence, otherwise "unknown" (never "missing").
- **Not built (optional in DESIGN 6.7).** Recommendation ids `air:*` (the site tasks carry the human steps) and the autopilot AIR cost row.
