### How `diagnose` classifies a cell

**Rule order:**

1. `unmeasured` when n < 4. A thin engine (Claude, or one with no answer in 28 days) uses n < 2 over 56 days.
2. **S0 blocked:** robots.txt blocks the engine's search bot, or the engine is listed in `blocked_engines`.
3. **S0 js_only:** only for fetchers that do not render (chatgpt, chatgpt_api, perplexity, claude). The mapped page has under 150 raw words, and either its rendered text is at least 3x longer or its issues say it needs JavaScript.
4. **S0 not_indexed**, Google group: the mapped page is `not_indexed` / `excluded`, or the site has no indexed page. Search Console impressions for a fan-out query, or any ranking reading, override this.
5. **S0 not_indexed**, Copilot: the site is not in the Bing account, or a page was submitted at least 14 days ago and is still not crawled.
6. **S5 / S4 / S3** from the answers:
   - The win thresholds are as in DESIGN 2.B.
   - A wrong claim, or negative sentiment in at least 25% of the named answers, demotes a win to S4 `wrong` / `negative`.
   - `low_position` means named often but with a median position above 3.
7. **S2:** ChatGPT read the page (`retrieved_json`; a citation counts as read), with basis `direct`. Or the client ranks in the top 10 of the engine's index for a fan-out query or the keyword, with basis `proxy_rank`:
   - Google / Perplexity: the SERP cache
   - Copilot: Bing positions
   - Claude: Brave
8. **S1:** `direct` (ChatGPT readings), `proxy_rank` (a reading where the client is not in the top 10) or `unknown_index`.

**Question-level signals:**
- `parametric_share`: answers with no citation and no search. `no_search` is set at 0.6 or more, with at least 3 answers.
- `list_dominated`: at least 40% of at least 3 citations go to lists or directories.
- `nobody_named`: at most 20% of answers name any business.
- `best_stage`: the highest stage held by engines carrying at least a quarter of the measured engine weight (2.E weights).
- The first and last dates named or cited.

### The source graph (`sources`)

Each row carries:
- `influence` = sum over questions of `V(q) x cites / answers`.
- Placeability as in the 2.C table.
- Page fetch result:
  - a 403 page has `names_client` = null
  - a stored reading is carried forward when the page was not fetched
  - a page that already names the client gets placeability 0, because the job is to keep that

Pages seen only in storage are kept with 0 cites for up to 120 days. LLM Mentions pages are added with `from: 'llm_mentions'`.

Rows are sorted by influence x placeability.

### Evidence (`rollEvidence`)

- The format is compact and opaque.
- Per-day cells cover 0-28 days and per-week cells 29-120 days. Each cell holds 11 counts.
- It also keeps fan-out queries, the keys of answers already counted (so it is idempotent), and the first named / last named / last cited dates.
- Anything older than 120 days is dropped.
- It is capped at 8,000 characters, trimming the least useful parts first.

### Fields added beside the frozen shapes (additive)

Nobody needs these, and nothing may rely on their absence:
- `corePanel.core[]`: `intent`, `baseline`, `cluster`, `market`. These feed `assignArms`.
- `mde`: `first_appearance_p`.
- `diagnose`: `mapped_page`, `fanout`, and `thin` on each cell.
- `sources[]`: `page_why`, `first_seen`.

### Inputs P4 must pass

- `assignArms` also takes `site_id` and `holdout_days`.
- `rollEvidence` also takes `prompt_id` and `ourHosts`.
- `corePanel`'s `demand` is `{ byPrompt: { prompt_id: { volume } }, candidates: [{ keyword, question?, source, volume, market }] }`. A plain `{ prompt_id: volume }` map is also accepted.
- `index.gsc` (an optional map of query to impressions) is read when present.
- `diagnose` indexes `answers8` / `units60` by question once per list (WeakMap). **P4 must pass the same arrays on every call** for the speed below.
- `sources` should get **28 days of answers**: `seo_ai_answers` keeps 35, and the name `answers8` is the contract's. It filters to 28 days itself.

Each labelled stage cell is one clear situation, wrapped in the noise a naive reader gets wrong:
- other engines' answers
- another question's answers that name the client
- `gemini_api` rows that name it
- named answers older than the window
- the client's host written with www or as a brand alias
- a look-alike host (`notcase-co.example`)
- unrelated citations
- a Google reading for an unrelated search where the client ranks first

The figures measure the kit's reading of the stored shapes and its edge handling. They do **not** measure agreement with real answers. That is DESIGN 7.2 stage 1 (60-80 hand-labelled production cells, target 85% exact / 95% on the S0-S1 boundary), and it needs the private dump.

## Production replay (2026-11-06, after merging `air/p4`)

P4 replayed the private dump with ARD + ARL. The tool is `harness/ai_rank_replay.js`; the outputs are private.

### What went wrong
1. **devaicon.com's core held 1 question.**
2. **Every arm was treated on both sites**, so there was no control group.

### Root causes (counts only, read from the dump)

**1. The panel had drifted to another country.**
- devaicon.com has 19 prompt rows:
  - 9 active, all PK-en
  - 9 retired, all AE-en (retired on 10-27)
  - the brand question
- The site's market is AE, correctly: it comes from the client's keywords. ARD's A3 rule only takes active questions of the site market, so nothing qualified. Only the retired question with a hit stayed, through the "never retired while it names you" path.
- Nothing ARD can do makes the retired AE questions asked again: AIR never activates a question.

**2. The strata were too thin.**
- techand.ai: 13 core questions over 7 clusters x 2 intents x 2 baselines gave 10 strata of 1-2 questions.
- The T,T,C pattern needs 3 questions in a stratum before it places a control.

**Also:** the real prompt rows use the kinds `choose`, `cost` and `problem`, which the fixtures do not have. They only matter when a question has fewer than 4 answers.

### Fixes in ARD

1. **`corePanel` fills up to `cfg.core_min`** (default 12, never above `core_max`) with the questions that are actually asked, when too few questions of the site market are.
   - These questions are labelled "outside your market (PK, yours is AE): measured because too few questions of your market are asked".
   - Progress can only be measured on questions somebody asks.
   - `cfg.core_min = 0` keeps DESIGN A3 to the letter.
2. **Retired questions of the site market are proposed again first.**
   - `source: 'retired_in_market'`, plus an additive field `revive` (the stored prompt id), so P4 / P6 can reactivate the row rather than write a duplicate.
   - This happens when fewer than `core_min` of the market's questions are asked.
3. **`assignArms` merges strata under 3 questions into a coarser stratum.**
   - It drops the cluster first, then the baseline, then the intent.
   - A new question joins its stored stratum: the computed one when it already holds stored questions, else the finest coarser level that does.
4. **`intentOf`'s fallback reads the kinds:** `choose` / `compare` mean recommendation; `cost` / `problem` mean informational.

## DESIGN 2.B deviation: provisional S0 / S1 with 0-3 answers (orchestrator decision, 2026-11-06)

### The decision

**Why.** A cold-start site (devaicon.com) needs eligibility and retrieval actions in week 1, not week 5. Those two stages do not depend on answer counts:
- robots.txt, the raw HTML and the index say whether an engine can read the client;
- a rank or fan-out proxy, or ChatGPT's own retrieved set, says whether it finds the client.

DESIGN 2.B's rule "unmeasured when n < 4" held them back on an 11-day-old programme.

**The rule now:**
- **S0 with fewer answers than needed** (0-3, or under 2 for a thin engine): assigned when its basis is robots, JavaScript or index presence. The cell is marked `provisional: true`.
- **S1 with fewer answers:** assigned only when it has a basis:
  - a results-page reading (`proxy_rank`), or
  - ChatGPT's retrieved set without the client (`direct`).

  It is never assigned on `unknown_index`. The cell is also `provisional: true`.
- **S2-S5 still need n >= 4.** With fewer answers, a cell that would be S2 or above stays `unmeasured / too_few`. That includes a citation or a naming, or a top-10 rank.
- **0 answers to this question:** provisional stages apply only when the site asks that engine at all. That means at least one answer to any question in 56 days. Otherwise the cell stays `unmeasured / not_asked`.
- **From n >= 4 on, the ordinary rules decide** and `provisional` is false. If they agree, the stage stays and simply loses the flag. If they disagree, the answer-based stage wins.
- **The evidence names the basis**, plus a line "provisional: n answer(s) in 28 days".

**What changes for other packages:** every cell now carries `provisional` (additive). P2 reads the stages; the coordinator is telling it about the flag.
