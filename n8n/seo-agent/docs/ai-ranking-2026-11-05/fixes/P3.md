## What changed, by finding

### F-08 (High): the headline pooled pulse answers to retired questions
- `v5/code/AI_Metrics.js:98-113`: a pulse morning's answers are kept only when their question id is on this run's panel (`p.prompts`); a run
  with no panel ids keeps every answer (as before). Readings left with no answer drop out of the week. The left-out answers are named in
  `pulse_basis.off_panel` (answers, readings, questions, ids) and in its note; with nothing left out the object is identical to before.
- `AI_Metrics.js:554`: the headline's buyer-question count is the panel questions with answers this week (run or pulse), not the run rows only.
- Comparability, `AI_Metrics.js:12-16, 449-452, 673`: DESIGN asked to bump `rule_version`. Bumping the stored rule (`AVM.RULE_VERSION`, in
  `_ai_stats.js`, not a P3 file) would also have dropped every stored pulse morning and every `units_json` from the 28-day windows, and it broke
  thirty legacy scenarios whose rows stand for the current rule. Instead `metrics_json` now carries `headline_rule: 'f08'`, and a week that
  actually left answers out is not compared with an earlier week that lacks the marker (reason: "measurement rule changed ... the earlier week
  counted such answers"). The next week compares again. A week with nothing left out counts the same answers under both rules, so it is
  compared as before. Known gap: a week whose own window has no off-panel answer, after a week that pooled some, is still compared.
- `harness/harness.js:117-121, 141, 146`: the harness's synthesized pulse mornings (fixture rows with no `detail_json`) now answer the site's own panel
  question ids (mocked prompts, or a hand-built plan's) instead of invented `pq_*` ids; without this S19 and several F1b / B8 scenarios read 0
  pulse answers under the fix.
- AI_Plan.js is untouched: it hands the mornings over; the filter sits where they are counted.

### F-37: AI Growth set aside the client's best-cited question
- `v5/code/Growth_Plan.js:50-63, 162-176, 206-215`. The shared fit gate is not changed (owner decision D13). Inside AI Growth:
  1. a question whose page is the client's own (AI cited it for this question, or a ladder / written page answers it) is never `set_aside`;
     the gate's finding stays in the reason and the item is listed as flagged-but-kept (`targets_set_aside[].kept = true`);
  2. a refusal for TOPIC only (`too broad`, `off topic`) is judged again on the keyword with buyers' abbreviations written out (ms 365, m365,
     o365, ms dynamics / teams / azure / power bi) and then on the full question without its scaffolding ("is", "or do I need", "for my");
     a refusal for a brand, a government body or a portal is never rescued. A synonym-rescued keyword is stored written out.
- Note for the integrator: in the gate "microsoft" is a category word, so "ms 365" and "microsoft 365" read the same there; the synonyms
  matter when the rest of a keyword decides. The production case (target 18) is covered by rules 1 and 2 (the full question passes).
- The Content Cadence still gates each AI target's keyword with the global gate, so a question kept by rule 1 or rescued on the full question
  can be listed as refused at writing time. That is the owner's rule (the global gate does not change); P6 may want to pass the rescue on.

### F-38: "not updated in 90+ days" on unread or undated pages
- `Growth_Plan.js:151-153, 178-180, 216`: a page's date is the newest of the content log's `published_at` and the date read on the page. No date
  and read: "no visible update date on the page". Never read: only "AI-readiness not checked yet". Old: "not updated in 90+ days (last date
  YYYY-MM-DD)". A page the agent published inside `refresh_after_days` and not read yet is `skipped` with "its AI readiness is checked before
  any refresh is planned" (it is read this week; a weak reading queues it next week).
- `v5/code/Cadence_Plan.js:428-430` (the line-440 filter): a page the agent published in the last 60 days counts as recently touched, like a page
  it refreshed, so no AI refresh of it is queued. **`app/server/src/services/cadence.ts:790` mirrors that filter and is P6's file: P6 must add
  the same line** (`refreshedRecently` + content-log rows with `published_url` and `published_at` < 60 days), or the app's preview can show an
  AI refresh the engine will not write.

### F-53: headings, prices, places and regulators counted as competitors
- `v5/code/_ai_entities.js:84-87, 106-160, 244, 253-256, 299-310, 322-339, 344, 381, 394`:
  - `junkName`: money and price ranges, dates and quarters, plan steps ("Phase 2", "Level 3"), answer words ("Yes"), a free zone named alone,
    places, regulator acronyms, and names made only of category or heading words (a new `HEADING_WORDS` list); a company suffix after a compound
    word ("Infotech Solutions") stays a name. `nameLike` refuses junk; a stored or learned junk name (the `ai_brands:` / `ai_entities:` book) is
    never scanned.
  - a business signal for a name nothing else knows (no domain, not configured, not learned): a company suffix, a brand-shaped word (eduNEXT,
    Folio3), business words in its own description, the engine's own brand list, a table whose first column header names businesses, or a list
    introduced as businesses ("A few options in the market:"). Otherwise it is `other`, not `competitor`.
  - tables: the header row is never a name; rows under a non-business first column ("Factor", "Feature") are not names.
  - a bold label ending in a colon is a name only with a "(by X)" owner, a suffix, a brand shape or a business-list intro, and a business
    description; numbered bold items ("3. Cubite") lose the number.
  - the link on a list item to a platform or directory page (openedx.org/partners/...) is never the item's website: this is what made openedx.org
    devaicon's "top rival". Open edX and Moodle are platforms (`OPEN_PLAT`: never a competitor unless configured); "SAP S/4HANA Cloud",
    "Business Central Premium", "Copilot AI" are their platform; more directories; a domain the client's own 2-3 word phrases name is its platform.
- Measured on the private production answers (443 rows, both sites, read locally, not stored): competitor entities 2,216 -> 1,729; those
  that are not domains 769 -> 330. The removed ones are almost all junk; a few real names went with them (e.g. a "313 ..." company whose name is
  a number plus a heading word). Synthetic labelled set in AF4: precision 100%, recall 100% (14 answers, 12 rivals).
- Existing entity accuracy set (AIENT, 60 cases): unchanged, no new miss.
- Purge: `fix_ai_entities_cache.py` (dry run by default, `--apply`, `--site`, `--self-test`). It reads `GEN`, `HEADING_WORDS`, `STOPW` and
  `DENY` from `_ai_entities.js` (one list, two readers) and mirrors the few patterns; `harness/accuracy/ai_entities_junk.json` (80 names)
  holds both to the same answers (AF4 runs it through the engine, `--self-test` through the script). On the private snapshot the dry run
  selects exactly rows 276 and 280 (the two the review named). **Owner step after deploy: run it dry, then `--apply`.**

### F-81: every AI report described the old schedule
- `v5/code/AI_Report.js:52-75, 229`: the schedule sentence of "How this is measured" is built from the run: the website's cycle day (its
  cycle run's date and time zone; "once a week, on this website's own weekly cycle day" when the run has no cycle), the weekly and monthly
  engines of this run, and whether the daily pulse is on, with the owner's `ai_pulse_engines` (read from `Load Monitors`) or the default set,
  and the rotating sample when the panel is large. The rest of the paragraph is unchanged.

### F-85: `serp:*` rows have an empty site id
- `build_monitors.py:403-406, 496-499`: `Load SERP Cache (AI)`, `Blank Old SERP Cache (AI)` and `Prune Old SERP Cache (AI)` match on kind and
  date only. Prune / blank now clean every site's rows older than 2 days (a reading only counts on its own day). Two legacy assertions that
  encoded the site filter were updated: `harness/scenarios_rengine.js:276-277`, `harness/scenarios_w2e3.js:270`.
  **For P4 / P5:** AIR's `Load SERP Cache (Rank)` reads `serp:` rows "of any date"; the AI visibility run prunes rows older than 2 days (it did
  so before too, for runs that name no site), so AIR sees at most about two days of readings.

## On-mode guards (no-ops while no row carries the new values)
- `Growth_Plan.js:66-70, 149-150, 162, 225, 239-240, 324`: `airOn = settings.rank.mode === 'on'` (undefined = off, CONTRACTS §4). On: target
  rows with `origin = 'ai_rank'` are skipped in both loops (AIR writes them) and their pages are reserved; their pages become key pages
  ("AI ranking target"). The plan carries `air_on: true` only when on, so an off plan is unchanged. Off / shadow: those rows are processed as
  AI Growth rows and `AI Target Rows` writes origin / action_id / live_at null (the rollback, D11).
- `Earned_Media.js:37-40, 72, 123`: on (`air_on`), a host with an `ai_source` prospect row of origin `ai_rank` gets no row, no draft, and a
  correction for it is left to AIR.
- `_ai_focus.js:186-190, 211-245`: a question whose `core_until` is in the future is never retired (market, off-focus, paused, over-quota,
  swap donor). Regenerated `app/shared/src/ai-focus.generated.js` (the build; the app parity test compares it byte for byte).
- `_ai_measure.js:293-295`: an action's time is `live_at || written_at || won_at`.
