### Per answer (`seo_ai_answers`, weekly and pulse)

| column | source |
|---|---|
| `answer_text` | The text the parser already reads. Runs of spaces collapse to one and runs of line breaks to two, so lists survive. Capped at 12,000 characters. |
| `cited_json` | `[{url, host, entity_id, role, position}]`, in the order the engine gives, one entry per page. URLs lose their tracking parameters. The entity id and role come from F1a's `AIE.read` entities, matched with `AIE.sameEntity`, so cleartax.in is cleartax.com. A host no entity covers keeps its registrable domain with role `source`. Your own pages carry role `self`. |
| `retrieved_json` | `[{url, host, entity_id}]`: the ChatGPT interface's `search_results` minus its cited pages, capped at 40. On the real `scraper.json` answer all 22 search results count as read and not cited, beside 7 cited pages. None of the 22 is one of the 7 once tracking parameters are removed: the Ministry page was read in Arabic (`/ar/`) and cited in English (`/en/`). |
| `fanout_json` | `fan_out_queries`, deduplicated, at most 20. |
| `products_json` | `[{title, merchant, price, currency, url, position, entity_id, own}]`. ChatGPT: `items[type=chat_gpt_products].items` (merchant comes from `merchants`, the price is a float). AI Mode and AI Overview: `ai_overview.items[type=ai_overview_shopping].items` (merchant from `seller`, then `marketplace`; the price is `price.current`). `own` means the card's domain is the site's domain, or the merchant is one of the brand names. |
| `ads_json` | `[{advertiser, url, host, rendered}]`. ChatGPT: `items[type=chat_gpt_ad]`, where `rendered` is `is_rendered`. AI Mode: `ai_overview_paid` elements, where `rendered` is true. |
| `local_json` | `[{name, url, host, position, own}]` from ChatGPT's `chat_gpt_local_businesses`. |
| `model` | `result.model` (scraper) or `result.model_name` (LLM Responses); `''` when the engine reports none (AI Mode, AI Overview). |
| `detail_json` | `{v, answer_len, answer_cut?, analysed?}` |

**`''` versus `'[]'`.** An empty list (`'[]'`) means the engine can show this and showed none. An empty string (`''`) means the engine does not
report it, or the row is older. This is what lets the shopping figures say "no shopping results in N answers" instead of 0%.

- Local cards and pages read: ChatGPT (scraper) only.
- A failed request stores `''` everywhere.

## 2. The analyst re-reads stored answers (F2's open item)

**The fix.**
- `Analyst_Jobs.js:15-24`: in a run's **last part** (`chunk.going && chunk.final_expected`), the stored rows of **this** run join the pool when
  they have `answer_text`, no error, and no `detail_json.analysed`. They are read from `Load AI Run Answers`.
- The pool is picked as before: answers naming the business or the brand question first, then recommend, compare, choose, custom and shopping
  answers. The last part's analyst budget caps the pool, and nothing is asked again.
- Each job carries `stored_ids`.
- `Apply_Analysis.js:16-20`: the stored rows those jobs name are read back with `AAD.fromStored`, which turns the strings back into booleans
  and numbers and takes the text from `answer_text`.
- They go through the same merge as the part's own rows. They come out flagged `_stored`, so AI Metrics counts their money once
  (`exec_cost` skips `_stored`).
- `Apply_Analysis.js:43`: every answer the analyst read gets `detail_json.analysed: true`.
- `AI Analysed Rows` writes them back, and `Update AI Answers` upserts on run + question + engine.

**Rows left alone.**
- An answer already read.
- A row from another run.
- A row stored before W2-E1 (it has no text).
- Any row in a part that is not the last.

`AI_Plan.js:204`: the `unanalysed_note` now says the last part reads those answers from their stored text, as far as its budget allows.

## 3. The visibility row (`seo_ai_visibility`)

The hook is `AI_Metrics.js:634-642`: `AAD.aggregate` runs over the run's buyer-question answers (`R`), covering this part and the earlier
parts' stored rows. Its rival set is `rivalSet` plus the competitor table. Its history is the site's earlier rows from `Load AI Visibility`.

**`urls_json`** has three parts:
- `own[]`, one entry per page of yours:
  - `cited_n`, and `retrieved_n` = the answers that read the page (pages read plus pages cited, so retrieved is never below cited);
  - `cited_rate` = cited out of read, with `rate_n`; `answers_n`;
  - `engines`, `questions`;
  - `rival_cited_instead`: the rival pages cited in the answers that read your page and did not cite it.
- `rivals[]`: rival pages AI cites.
- `retrieved_not_cited[]`: your pages read and not cited, with `top_competing`, the pages cited in their place.

If no engine of the run reports what it read, a `note` says so.

**`sources_json`** = `{v: 2, n, answers_n, citing_answers_n, types: {own, rival, ugc, reference, editorial, government, directory, other: {n, share}}, domains: [...], history_runs, method, list}`.
- Each domain has `{host, type, cited_n, share, engines, first_seen, last_seen, half_life_days, decay, weeks}`.
- Shares count citations: each answer counts a domain once.
- `first_seen` is carried from the previous stored row. A domain not cited this run keeps its dates for 120 days.
- **Half-life**:
  - It needs 3 or more stored runs over 14 days or more. The figure is the days from the domain's peak share to half of that peak,
    interpolated between runs.
  - `decay` reads `halved`, `holding`, `at its peak now` or `too little history`.
  - With fewer runs, `half_life_days` is `null`.
- **`list` is the old array** (`[{domain, citations, engines, topics, kind, urls}]`). The column used to hold that array, so an older row holds
  an array and a new row holds this object.

**`shopping_json`.**
- `status: "measured"` carries:
  - `trigger_rate` and its `trigger_ci` (Wilson), over `answers_n`, the answers on engines that can show products;
  - `products_n`;
  - `own_presence_rate` with `own_presence_n`, and `own_avg_position`;
  - `merchants` (share of cards), the top `products`, and `by_engine`.
- `status: "no shopping results"` sets `trigger_rate: null` (never 0) and gives a `note` with n.

**`ads_local_json`** = `{ads: {seen_n, rendered_n, answers_n, rate, advertisers}, local: {seen_n, own_n, answers_n, rate, own_rate, rivals}}`.
When nothing was seen, the rate is `null` and the n is still given.

**Action.** `AI_Metrics.js:640` adds one action, "Make AI cite <page> where it already reads it". It fires when a page was read without being
cited in 2 or more answers, and it names the page cited instead.

## 4. Report and callback

`AI_Report.js:155` calls `AAD.reportHtml`. Every rate is printed with its n.
- **Pages AI reads but does not cite**: your page, how many answers read it, how many cited it, and the page cited instead.
- **Where AI gets its answers**: kinds of source with their share, then the top sites with kind, cited-in count, first seen and "its share
  halved in N days" (or the reason there is no figure).
- **AI shopping results**: only when cards appeared. Rate, 95% range, your products and their card position, and sellers by share of cards.
- **Ads inside AI answers** and **Local business cards in AI answers**: only when seen.

The callback (`Build_AI_Callback.js`, unchanged) passes `answer_data` through with the rest of the report item. W2E1-5 checks that it arrives.

## 5. Panel: the "shopping" stage

- `Prompt_Jobs.js:34-61`: `AAD.sellsProducts` reads the site's pages (`Load Pages (AI)`), its business type and its services. A site sells
  products when any of these holds:
  - 3 or more product pages (`/products/`, `/shop/`, `/collections/`, `/p/`…);
  - a shop business type plus one product page or one product service;
  - 2 product services.
- Only then do the writer jobs carry `shopping: true` and `shopping_why`. Every other site's jobs are byte-for-byte as before (W2E1-6 checks
  techand.ai).
- `ai_prompts.txt`: the shopping kind and stage lines are rendered only when `$json.shopping` is set.
- `Prompt_Rows.js:27-28` accepts the kind and stage `shopping`, `_ai_panel.js` `stageOf` accepts the stage, and Analyst Jobs picks shopping
  answers.

Weekly engines (5 per question): 13.8 KB a question before, 34.9 KB after. Pulse (ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Mode): 7.7 KB before, 21.7 KB after,
per question per morning.

| panel | weekly run, MB a week | pulse, MB a week (at most) | retained before | retained, untrimmed | **retained with the trim** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 q | 0.69 → 1.74 | 2.3 → 6.5 (6 mornings) | 19 MB | 49 MB | **35 MB** |
| 200 q | 2.76 → 6.97 | 9.2 → 26.0 (≤ 6 mornings) | 75 MB | 197 MB | **138 MB** |
| 500 q | 6.90 → 17.44 | 11.5 → 32.5 (≤ 3 mornings, 300 q each) | 153 MB | 396 MB | **279 MB** |

The pulse column is an upper bound: in auto mode, a large panel asks each question on fewer mornings. Rows keep today's retention: 120 days
weekly, 21 days pulse.

**Without a trim, one 200-question site would reach n8n's default 200 MB** for all Data Tables together, and F2 then stops runs with
`failed_store`. Two update nodes, built in `build_monitors.py:427-433` and `:513-515`, now blank `answer_text` and `retrieved_json`.

| node | rows it blanks | when |
|---|---|---|
| `Trim Old Answer Text` (weekly) | this site's non-pulse rows aged 35 to 49 days (a missed week is caught the next week) | after `Prune Old Answers`, before the brief |
| `Trim Pulse Answer Text` | pulse rows aged 7 to 9 days | after `Prune Pulse Answers` |

- Both run once, with continue on error. The rows themselves stay.
- 35 days is five weekly runs, far beyond the analyst's 6.2-day re-read.
- The n8n update node (2.41.4 source, `common/addRow.js`) passes the `defineBelow` values as given, so `''` is written. The update returns the
  rows it changed into the execution, bounded by the window (one site, two weeks).
- A 500-question panel still needs `N8N_DATA_TABLES_MAX_SIZE_BYTES` raised. MARKET_RND gap 15 already says so, and before W2-E1 it held
  153 MB.
