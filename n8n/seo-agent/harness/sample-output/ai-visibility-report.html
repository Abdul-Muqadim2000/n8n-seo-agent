How this is measured

Every Monday the same buyer questions are asked to ChatGPT (the real chatgpt.com answer), Perplexity, Gemini and Claude with web search, and to Google AI Mode; the matching Google search is checked for an AI Overview. Rates and share of voice use the buyer questions only; the question about the brand itself measures recognition. "Named" = the answer mentions northwind-erp.com / Northwind ERP / northwind-erp; "cited" = it links one of your pages; share of voice = your mentions against the competitors' mentions; position = your place among the businesses an answer names. Answers vary between runs, so read the trend over several weeks. Cost this run: $0.58 (DataForSEO).
